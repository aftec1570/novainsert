NovaInsert @VERSION@ is installed in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/NovaInsert.vst3.

In REAPER: Options → Preferences → Plug-ins → VST → Re-scan, then add VST3: NovaInsert to a track. Open SETUP to add your outboard devices, start at low monitoring levels, and never calibrate into a monitoring path.
