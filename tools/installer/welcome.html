NovaInsert @VERSION@

This installer places the NovaInsert VST3 plug-in in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, where REAPER finds it automatically.

NovaInsert is a REAPER-only hardware insert plug-in for macOS (Apple silicon): sample-accurate latency measurement, explicit mono/stereo routing, a device library and send/return VU meters.
